### How to use it

- `sc_send` is a one-way channel to their phone: the notification lands on their phone and leaves nothing in this conversation.
- The title is the only line the notification shows, at most 32 characters; the body is Markdown and can hold links and steps.
- The same title and body is refused within 60 seconds; never send it twice just to check that you are online.
- Server酱³ keys take tags; Turbo keys do not, and the receipt says when they were left out.
- A successful push only means Server酱 accepted it, not that they have seen it.
